      How it works

      You can start in under a minute. The rest of this page is optional.

      Make your first video

      1. Write or paste your text

        Anything works: a full script, a blog post, a story, or a few sentences. You do not need to format it.

      2. Choose how it looks and sounds

        Pick a voice, turn captions on or off, and choose landscape or vertical. In a hurry? Press one of the Quick start buttons.

      3. Press Generate video

        It usually takes one to three minutes. When it is ready you can watch it, download it or delete it.

      Go to Create

      Want more control? Write it as scenes

      This is optional. If you add a few labelled lines, the video follows your plan exactly.

      Write thisWhat it does
      Narrator: Every dog has a dream.The voice reads these words aloud.
      Visual: A dog on a trampolineDescribes what should be on screen.
      Keywords: dog, trampolineSearch words used to find footage.
      Style: cartoonLooks for animated clips first. Leave it out for real footage.

      Tips for better videos
      
        
        
          
      • Describe things a camera can film, like "a dog running in a garden". Ideas like "success" are harder to show.
        • 
          
      • One idea per scene works best. Short sentences make cleaner captions.
        • 
          
      • Try two or three scenes first to see if you like the footage, then make the full video.
        • 
          
      • Longer scripts take longer to make. A 60 to 90 second video is a good size.
        • 
          
      • You can change the voice, captions or look and generate again. Your earlier videos stay in the Library.
